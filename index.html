What We Do

Plumbing Services

Practical plumbing repairs, fittings, and maintenance for homes, apartments, and workplaces.

Pipe Repair & Replacement

Repairing cracked, leaking, or corroded water supply and drainage pipes, as well as replacing worn pipe sections.

Tap & Faucet Repair

Fixing dripping taps, worn washers, stiff spindles, and replacing kitchen, utility, or washbasin mixers.

Bathroom & Toilet Plumbing

Servicing flush tanks, cisterns, health faucets, shower mixers, angle valves, and washbasin connections.

Leakage Detection & Repair

Tracing and sealing water seepage around pipe joints, wall connections, sink traps, and overhead tank lines.

Sink & Drain Unclogging

Clearing slow or blocked kitchen sinks, bathroom floor traps, bottle traps, and household drain outlets.

Fixture & Appliance Installation

Neat installation of new sinks, commodes, showers, water purifier inlets, and washing machine connections.
